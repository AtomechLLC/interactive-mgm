building blocks: noise · Fire Emblem

The screen says 80%. The game rolls 92%.

After the Game Boy Advance Fire Emblem games (Intelligent Systems, 2002 to 2004): a hit lands when the average of two rolls from 0 to 99 is below the displayed hit. The curve is computed exactly from all 10,000 pairs; the 100 attacks come from a seeded generator.

building blocks: noise · XCOM

Same 65% shot, shorter losing streaks

After XCOM: Enemy Unknown (Firaxis, 2012), whose lower difficulties quietly raise the player's hit chance after misses. The size of the bonus here is illustrative. Twenty squads each take twelve 65% shots from one seed, with and without the bonus.
