Behind the screen

Who swings

After Fire Emblem: The Binding Blade, The Blazing Blade and The Sacred Stones (Intelligent Systems, 2002 to 2004): the hit roll is the average of two random numbers from 0 to 99, compared with the displayed hit. The percentages here are counted exactly from that rule. The portraits of Lyn and Batta are the game's; Pip and Rook stand in for them on the field.

Miss twice and the game quietly helps

Shot on screen

Difficulty

The real number

After XCOM: Enemy Unknown (Firaxis, 2012): on Easy and Normal, the game's settings files hold a hidden aim bonus for each miss in a row on shots shown at 50% or better. Classic and Impossible roll what is shown. We use 15 points per miss, capped at 30. The Sectoid portrait is the game's; Kit is the soldier and Grub the alien.
