three ways to grow toughness · Sekiro

One enemy, two bars to empty

After Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice (FromSoftware, 2019): attacks and deflects fill the enemy's posture bar, a full bar opens a deathblow, and posture recovers more slowly as vitality drops. The enemy swings every two seconds; all damage and posture numbers here are illustrative.

three ways to grow toughness · Halo

Same 115 points, one part comes back

After Halo: Combat Evolved (Bungie, 2001): shields refill after a short delay without damage, while health under them only returns with a health pack. Both soldiers face the same five firefights; the pool sizes, delay and damage are illustrative.
