building blocks: noise · Tetris

Same pieces, same average, different droughts

Deal

Pure random gives each of the seven pieces equal odds every time. NES Tetris (1989) rolls once more when it would repeat the last piece. Modern Tetris shuffles a bag of all seven, deals it out, then fills a new bag, so the longest possible wait for an I is 12 pieces. The wells are a toy model of our own: each other piece adds four cells to a nine-wide stack and an I clears four rows. Sequences come from a seeded generator.
