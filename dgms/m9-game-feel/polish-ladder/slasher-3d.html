Slasher · polish presets

A three.js rebuild of Andre’s Unity slasher. Each key loads the build’s preset with the same values. Every preset adds to the one before it.

Rendering is turned up past the build: image-based lighting, a PBR stone floor, bloom, chromatic aberration on hits, a film grade, and shader-driven slashes, shockwaves and dissolves.
