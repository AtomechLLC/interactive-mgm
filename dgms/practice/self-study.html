Self-Study Project

Seventeen parts that grow one game, from your own pillars to a vertical slice. Each part has the exercise, the optional template, and the interactives to open while you work. Tick a part off when you post it; your progress stays in this browser.

How each part works

  1. Do the exercise with your team, or alone.
  2. Use the blank template if it helps: every one is optional.
  3. Post the result to your Discord thread and tag the instructors.
  4. Fold what you learn into your game design document as you go.

Where the parts come from

Part 1, Craft Your Own Pillars, is written for this course. Parts 2 to 17 are the project from Mastering Game Mechanics, with its worked example, Echidna Z, a “playful soulslike”. Their interactives are that course’s pages, and each part names the MGM module it draws on.

Open “Worked example” on a part to see a finished answer.
