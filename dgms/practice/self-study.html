Self-Study Project

Eighteen parts that direct one game, and one moment in it, from your own pillars to a polish priority list. Each part picks up where that day’s workshop stopped and adds one page to your Director’s Brief. Tick a part off when you post it; your progress stays in this browser.

How each part works

  1. Read what the workshop left undone, then do the exercise with your team.
  2. Write the page named under “Add to your Director’s Brief”.
  3. Post it to your Discord thread and tag the instructors.
  4. From Part 6 on, work on one signature action: the single thing your character does that the game is known for.

Working alone or without a concept? Run the same steps on a published game you know well, and write the brief as if you were its director.

Echidna Z, the running example

Echidna Z is a “playful soulslike”: a down-on-his-luck echidna who claws, burrows and deflects his way to the throne. Mastering Game Mechanics designed his mechanics. Here the team directs his signature action, Burrow, from pillar to polish list.

Core verbs: Slash, Burrow, Jump, Deflect. Partner: Snapdragonella, a flying ambusher. Open “Worked example” on any part to see a finished page.
