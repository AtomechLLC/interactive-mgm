The four classes

Characterrepresents the character’s actions · demonstrates traits · stands out
Environmentindicates danger · defines ambience · comprised of many parts
UI / UXguides non-diegetically · shows how players should feel · marks success or failure
Musicdefines tone · provides context · notifies changes in gameplay state

Watch the clip twice: once for what you see, once with your eyes closed. The second pass finds the environment and UI sounds you stopped noticing.
