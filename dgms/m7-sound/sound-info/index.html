After Overwatch (Blizzard, 2016): every ultimate has a voice line heard across the map, and most heroes have two, one the enemy team hears and one their own team hears. The four pairs in the table are the game's. The street, the timings and the ledge are our own. Kit plays Hanzo; Pip and Sam are your team; Rook and Bram are the other one.

sound design · four classes sorter · Left 4 Dead

Every special infected announces itself

Name it from the sound

Press a name, or 1 to 5, to start the quiz. Turn the sound on to hear our stand-in for each cue.

After Left 4 Dead (Valve, 2008): each special infected has a distinct sound that plays before it is in view, and the Tank has its own music. The descriptions and the advice follow the game. The synthesised cues are rough stand-ins for the real recordings, and the corridor is our own. Wisp plays the Hunter, Sam the Smoker, Bram the Boomer, Grub the Tank and Sage the Witch; Pip and Kit are the survivors.

After Counter-Strike (Valve, 2000 onward): running footsteps can be heard by other players at a distance, and holding walk makes them silent at roughly half speed (about 52% in Global Offensive). The corridor, the hearing distance and the clock are our own. Pip is attacking; Rook is holding the site.
