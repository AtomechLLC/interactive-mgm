sound design · hear the boss coming · Overwatch

A loud line is a warning and a rally call

After Overwatch (Blizzard, 2016): every hero shouts a voice line when their ultimate starts, loud enough to carry across the fight, and some heroes say one line to their own team and a different one to the enemy. The heroes, lines and reaction times here are made up for the demo; the two maps run the same fight with and without the shout.

sound design · four classes sorter · Left 4 Dead

Every special infected announces itself

After Left 4 Dead (Valve, 2008): each special infected has its own sound, such as the Smoker's cough, the Boomer's gurgle, the Hunter's growl and the Witch's crying, and the Tank arrives with its own music. Survivors often hear a special long before they see it. Distances, speeds and the synthesised cues here are illustrative.

sound design · same scene, four rules · Counter-Strike

Running is loud, walking is slow

After Counter-Strike (Valve, from 1999): running makes footsteps that enemies can hear and locate; holding the walk key makes you silent but roughly half as fast. Speeds follow the familiar 250 units a second running with a knife and about 130 walking; the hearing range here is illustrative.
