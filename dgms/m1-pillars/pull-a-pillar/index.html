Pillars as restraints

Twelve move ideas, two filters

A restraint (“The game must have combos”) lets through anything that is literally a combo, mashing included. A pillar (“Controlled Power”) describes a feel, so it lets in charge attacks, parries and a held-back super, and turns away button-mash spam.
