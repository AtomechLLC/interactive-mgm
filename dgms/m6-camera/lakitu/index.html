The camera is a character with a job

Lakitu's brief (R button)

Q E are C left and C right. W A S D drive Mario yourself.

After Super Mario 64 (Nintendo, 1996): the camera is presented as one of the Lakitu Bros. filming Mario, the C buttons ask him to circle, R switches between the wide Lakitu cam and the closer Mario cam, and the game buzzes when he cannot move. Lakitu's art is the game's. The courtyard, distances and turn rates are our own, and the right-hand view is a simple ray-cast sketch, not the game's renderer.
