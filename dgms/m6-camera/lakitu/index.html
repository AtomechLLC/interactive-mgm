camera rigs · Super Mario 64

The camera is a character with a job

After Super Mario 64 (Nintendo, 1996): the camera is presented as Lakitu filming Mario. The C buttons ask him to circle Mario, R switches between his wider, trailing shot and a closer Mario cam, and he can even be seen in a mirror. The level, distances and lag here are illustrative; the right-hand view is a simple ray-cast sketch, not the game's renderer.
