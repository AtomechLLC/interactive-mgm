controls as metaphor · Shadow of the Colossus

Holding R1 is holding on

Or hold R. Let go on a ledge to rest. Hold on when it shakes.

After Shadow of the Colossus (Team Ico, 2005): R1 grips while it is held, a circular grip gauge drains while you hang on, and colossi try to shake you off. Here holding also climbs, and the ledges, drain rates and shake times are our own. Pip plays Wander and Grub, at three times his size, plays the colossus.

controls as metaphor · Death Stranding

L2 and R2 are your two hands

A is L2, the left hand. D is R2, the right hand. Pull the strap opposite the lean. Hold both to brace.

After Death Stranding (Kojima Productions, 2019): L2 and R2 are Sam's left and right hands on his pack straps. One trigger shifts the load back when it tips, and holding both braces at a slower walk. The terrain, the tipping model and the speeds are our own. Pip carries the cargo.

controls as metaphor · Katamari Damacy

Two sticks, two hands on the ball

Left stick W S, right stick I K. Hold two at once.

After Katamari Damacy (Namco, 2004): both sticks forward rolls the ball ahead, one stick arcs it, and opposite sticks turn on the spot. Things smaller than the ball stick to it and make it grow. The lawn, the objects and the growth rate are our own.
