controls as metaphor · Shadow of the Colossus

Holding R1 is holding on

or hold the R key

After Shadow of the Colossus (Team Ico, 2005): R1 grips while held, a circular grip gauge drains while you hang on, and colossi try to shake you off. Here, holding also climbs, except during a shake. Ledges, drain rates and shake times are illustrative.

controls as metaphor · Death Stranding

L2 and R2 are Sam's two hands

or hold Q for L2 and E for R2

After Death Stranding (Kojima Productions, 2019): L2 and R2 grab Sam's left and right straps. A trigger pulls the load toward its side, so a lean to the left is caught with R2; holding both steadies him at a slower walk. Lean physics, bumps and speeds here are illustrative.

controls as metaphor · Katamari Damacy

Two sticks are two hands on the ball

or hold W / S for the left stick and I / K for the right

After Katamari Damacy (Namco, 2004): both sticks forward rolls ahead, both back rolls back, and one forward with one back turns, like pushing a ball with two hands. The room, objects and growth rule are illustrative.
