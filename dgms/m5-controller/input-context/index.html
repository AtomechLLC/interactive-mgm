3Cs: controller · interaction plus context · Batman: Arkham

One strike button, the stick picks who

Click in the arena to point the stick, then press Strike (or click again). With the stick centred, Strike picks the nearest thug.

After Batman: Arkham Asylum (Rocksteady, 2009) and its sequels: the strike button attacks whichever thug lies closest to the direction the stick points, leaping across the room if needed, so the combo flows from one target to the next. The scoring rule here (angle first, then distance) is a simplification.

Calling an airstrike is a test of nerve

or type with W A S D, as on PC

After Helldivers 2 (Arrowhead, 2024): each stratagem is called by a short directional code entered while you hold the stratagem key, and a wrong direction clears the entry. Codes shown: Reinforce, Eagle Airstrike, Resupply and Orbital Precision Strike. The bugs and timings are illustrative.
