3Cs: controller · interaction plus context · Batman: Arkham

One strike button, the stick picks who

Point the stick with W A S D, or move the mouse over the arena. J or a click strikes.

After Batman: Arkham Asylum (Rocksteady, 2009) and its sequels: the strike button attacks whichever thug lies closest to the direction the stick points, leaping across the room if needed, so a combo flows from one target to the next. The scoring rule here (closest angle within a cone, nearest when the stick is centred) is a simplification. Pip plays Batman; Rook, Bram, Wisp and Sam play the thugs.
