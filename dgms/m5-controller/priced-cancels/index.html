3Cs: controller · cancel philosophies · Guilty Gear

Cancel almost anything, for half the bar

After Guilty Gear (Arc System Works, from Guilty Gear X, 2000): a Roman Cancel ends almost any move at once and costs 50% of the Tension bar, which fills as you attack. Here each attack string is four hits and a slow finisher; the defender gets a turn whenever a finisher is not cancelled. Tension gain and timings are illustrative.

3Cs: controller · cancel anti-patterns · Go

The ko rule puts a price on the loop

Go: a capture that would return the board to the position before the opponent's last move is not allowed at once. The player must play elsewhere first, usually a threat the opponent has to answer. The ko shape here is standard; the threats are placed for illustration.
