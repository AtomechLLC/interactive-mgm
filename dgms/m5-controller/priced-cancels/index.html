A rule written to stop an infinite loop

Ko rule

J plays the next move.

Go's ko rule: a player may not make a move that returns the board to the position before the opponent's last move. The ko in the middle is the standard shape; the other stones and the threats played "elsewhere" are placeholders of our own, not a real game record.
