Most designers are a mix. The point is to know which questions you lean toward, and apply for roles that use them.

The four types are Richard Carrillo's, from his book The Role of a Great Game Designer (2021) and his GDC 2015 talk "Designing Your Design Team". Names follow the updated book (Visionary became Director, Salesman became Architect). Where each type sits on the deck's axes is not confirmed yet.
