Swap the tool

Your tool

Pick a tool, then click a head.

One kind of reaction is a tool change: the fire head only answers to ice, the ice head to fire, the stone head to a blade or a bomb. A wrong tool bounces off with a ting, and the sound teaches the rule. The post counts this idea under about 90% of the arcade and platforming era.
