Judge a group game one seat at a time

Who decides

A board fight of up to 40 seconds, ten a side. You hold the gold seat. Under a leader, press Space to carry out each order. On your own, use the arrow keys.

Your seconds and decisions are your own play. The win rates are measured by this page: 300 matches per rule with a script in your seat, against an opposing script that hesitates on 10% of its turns, while your eight teammates also hesitate on 10% when left to themselves. Those two figures are this page's own. After the Karazhan chess event.
