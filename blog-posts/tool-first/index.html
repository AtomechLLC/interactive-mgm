Replanned · Nightbane and the tools post

Twenty seconds on a tool, or four more tests?

How it works

You have 60 seconds to fly the dragon through three rings by setting three numbers. A test flight costs 5 seconds. Or spend 20 seconds building a live preview, after which the path redraws as you change a number.

Keys: Q/A, W/S, E/D change the three numbers, Space tests, T builds the tool. Every run draws new rings. The rows are your own runs; the walk-through is a scripted designer at three times speed who changes one number at a time and keeps what helped; with the preview it tries every neighbouring setting. After the Nightbane flight path, tuned by hand without a path tool.
