Replanned · D&D Advantage

Advantage, or a flat +3?

How it works

Twelve checks. Each shows the number you need on a d20. Before the roll, pick Advantage (roll twice, keep the higher) or a flat +3. One pick per check, no take-backs.

Odds are exact: Advantage succeeds with 1 − ((need − 1) / 20)², +3 with (24 − need) / 20, capped at 0 and 100%. No automatic hit on a natural 20, as for a 5e ability check. The dice are real rolls. After D&D 5e and Baldur's Gate 3.
