The pity rule decides when you spend

How it works

Eight banners, one character each. Twelve pulls arrive with every banner. On each one you choose: pull now, or move on and keep the stash. You play the eight twice, and only the pity rule changes.

Every pull is a real roll at the stated rate, so luck moves the totals and one run is one sample. The pulls per banner are your own choices. The walk-through's player follows a written rule. After Genshin Impact and Honkai: Star Rail.
