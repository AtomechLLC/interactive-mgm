Which need sets the clock

Play one 36-second day with the arrow keys or WASD. Keep the three needs up and find the six landmarks before dusk.

The strips and counts are your own play. The drain rates are set by this page so that every setting drains the same total, and the day is compressed to 36 seconds. After Don't Starve, The Long Dark and Subnautica.
