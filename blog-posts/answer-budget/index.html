How it works

Pick three mechanics for a 30-second boss, then heal through it with keys 1, 2 and 3. Each mechanic is one of three kinds: react, prepare or recuperate.

Misses, presses and idle seconds are your own play. The walk-through runs a 22-second fight with a scripted healer. Mechanic names are in the style of Final Fantasy XIV raids and World of Warcraft Mythic+ affixes.
