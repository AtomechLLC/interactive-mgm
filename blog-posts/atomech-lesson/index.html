multi-actor gameplay

Call and Swap

Inputs

One Active unit takes the controller; the other two wait in the background. Call one in and it holds a share of the life pool for a few seconds. Call it again to swap. If its share runs out, it leaves early and that life is gone.

Rules from the Atomech “Multi-Actor Gameplay” design notes (shares: Echelon 20%, Coil 30%, Phalanx 40%). Call length, damage and what happens to a leftover share are this page’s reading. Icons: game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0) by Lorc and Delapouite.
