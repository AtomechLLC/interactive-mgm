Replanned · Invisible game mechanics

Did the game rob you, or did you miss?

How it works

Mario runs by himself; Space (or click) jumps. Same ten gaps three times, one hidden rule changed each run. After a fall, say whose fault it felt like.

Hidden rule: coyote time (a jump still fires just after you leave the ledge) plus the same frames of jump buffering: 0, 4 or 10 frames at 60 per second, shuffled. Counts are your own presses; windows are counted from this page's physics. Sprites: Super Mario Bros.
