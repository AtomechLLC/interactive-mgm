The creature strikes

Hold → to walk the corridor and Space to hide. You hear six growls on the way. A strike comes about two seconds after its growl and takes a heart unless you are hiding.

The purple bars are your own stops, hides and steps back, and one walk per setting is a small sample. Knowing the setting in advance also changes how you walk it.
