Replanned · XP Curves and What an RPG Needs

XP rules decide what grinding buys

How it works

Five areas, each with a boss. In every area you choose again and again: fight a monster for XP, or go at the boss. You play the campaign three times, and only the XP rule changes.

Nothing is rolled: fights run on level formulas written for this page. The walk-through's player follows a written rule. After Dragon Quest, the scaled XP of Pokémon Black and White, and the level scaling of The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion.
