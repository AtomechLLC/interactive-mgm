Replanned · Casual Game Design

Four easy levels. Where do they go?

How it works

Twelve short levels, four of them easy. First choose where the easy ones go by clicking the strip. Then play your sequence: click a tile, then a neighbour, to swap them into a line of three.

Easy: three colours, 3 lines in 7 moves. Hard: five colours, 4 lines in 6 moves. A swap that makes no line is a wasted move; running out of moves means trying the level again. The bars are your own play, and twelve levels from one person is a small sample. The walk-through's player is a script that never tires, so its bars show nothing about rest. After Candy Crush Saga and Angry Birds.
