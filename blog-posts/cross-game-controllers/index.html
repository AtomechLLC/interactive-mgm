Do What They Meant

The jump

Twenty players press jump around the right moment, a few frames early or late, the way real hands do. Coyote time still lets a jump out after you leave the ledge; the buffer remembers a press made just before landing.

Super Mario Bros. sprites (Nintendo, 1985) via mariouniverse.com. Press timings are a seeded toy model. Celeste’s Maddy Thorson documented both tricks publicly.

Same Camera, Three Games

Framing

All three are over-the-shoulder third-person combat games. Nudging the camera offset takes minutes in any engine; the effect on what the player can see, and so on the whole combat design, is deep.
