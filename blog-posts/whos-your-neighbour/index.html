Who gets the ore

Gather ore for 45 seconds with the arrow keys or WASD. Six other miners want it too. Play all three rules.

Your ore, the nodes taken from you and the nodes you shared are your own play. The other miners are scripts that walk to the nearest ore. The coin price is a simple formula, printed under the field. After Guild Wars 2, World of Warcraft Classic and New World.
