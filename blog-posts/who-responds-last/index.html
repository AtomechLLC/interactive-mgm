Replanned · Card Game Design

Mana you hold is mana you did not spend

How it works

Five turns, two mana each. Either play a creature or hold the mana for the instant in your hand. Spells resolve last in, first out: whatever was cast last happens first.

His untapped mana and cards in hand are what he could answer with. The cards are generic stand-ins for Magic: The Gathering's Lightning Bolt, Giant Growth and Counterspell. The table is your own five choices.
