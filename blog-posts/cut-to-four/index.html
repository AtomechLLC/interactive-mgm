A kit is also what it cannot do

How it works

Ten candidate abilities for an artillery mage. Keep four. The page then runs your kit against an assassin, a bruiser and a sniper, and shows whether each side got to play.

The duel is a small simulation written for this page, with its numbers on the cards, so the grid shows what these rules produce and not a real game's balance. A matchup counts as two-sided when the loser took off at least 35% of the winner's health. After the Xerath rework in League of Legends and Overwatch hero kits.
