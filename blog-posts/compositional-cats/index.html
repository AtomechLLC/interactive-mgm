interaction gives depth

Parts and Anchors

How pieces connect

Pieces never talk to each other directly, only through a shared anchor. Hover an anchor to see who reaches it. Click a piece to cut it: as long as its anchors have another source, nothing else breaks.

Pieces are the article’s examples (Cleric, Butcher, Tinker, Mage and items); the item names and the Mage’s exact spells are illustrative. Icons: game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0) by Lorc and Zeromancer.

cross-class synergy

One Bolt, Two Classes

Cast

Pick an element and click a tile. The Tinker’s whole class runs on one rule: a mechanical unit struck by electricity takes an extra turn. The Mage was never designed for the Tinker, but they share the electric anchor.

Reactions follow the game’s own element tips, collected on the element interactions page; damage numbers are illustrative. Icons: game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0) by Lorc, Delapouite and Sbed.
