Every freedom you leave in needs an answer

How it works

An open-world delivery mission. For each freedom, click no rule, a wall, a fail state or a recovery. Then send in seven playtesters who each have one habit.

The playtesters are scripts, and each result follows from your rules by fixed logic: run the same mission twice and you get the same seven outcomes. The point costs are this page's own. After the mission scripting of Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2.
