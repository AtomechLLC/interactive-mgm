The gain between hand and character

Gain: hand to character

Hold the mouse button on the canvas and drag: the character moves your hand's distance times the gain. Let go on a pad to land on it. Letting go within 40 px of a pad without being on it is a missed landing; letting go in open ground is only a new grip. Times, strokes and misses are your own; the walk-through is a scripted hand that stops within 8 pixels of where it aims. After Gorilla Tag's arm movement and mouse sensitivity in osu! and Valorant.
