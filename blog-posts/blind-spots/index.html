How it works

Click every game you have played enough to have opinions about. The bars show which genres and which of the five skill types your history covers. You are the whole dataset.

Sixty games, five per genre. Each is tagged with the two skill types it leans on most (Physical Execution, Decision Making, Processing, Knowledge, Freeform); the tags are this page's reading, not a measurement. The suggestions pick unplayed games from your thinnest genres and skill types. After Alex Brazie's play-history list.
