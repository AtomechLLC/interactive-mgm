Interface rules

A 40-second skirmish. Q builds a worker, W a soldier. Drag a box to select, click the ground to send. Hold your base and hurt the red camp.

Every count is your own play. The cap of four stands in for twelve at this scale, and the 40% larger raids under the new rules are this page's stand-in for an opponent who gets the same interface help. After StarCraft: Brood War's twelve-unit selection and manual worker rallies, and StarCraft II's unlimited selection.
