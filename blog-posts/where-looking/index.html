Where the dash cue lives

Steer with the mouse. Press Space the moment your dash turns red (or you hear it). Turn sound on for the last one.

Eight changes a round; an unnoticed change counts as 2.5 s. The times on the right are your own. After Celeste's hair (red with a dash, blue without), Halo's shield bar and the reload barks of F.E.A.R. and Gears of War.
