How many kits should a gate let through?

Tools that can open the gate

Set the looseness, then play three rooms. Each hands you two tools at random. Pick a tool, then click the prop it could work on.

The kit counts are exact for six tools taken two at a time. The times and tries are your own play, and three rooms is a small sample. After the rune and climbing kit of Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom.
