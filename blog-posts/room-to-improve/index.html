Replanned · Counter-strafing in tactical shooters

A rule that is hard to execute leaves room to get better

Accuracy rule

Hold D to step out, Space to fire. He fires 0.45 s after he sees you. Under the strict rule a shot only lands if you are nearly still: letting go of D slides to a stop in 0.3 s, tapping A stops you at once, which is the counter-strafe of Counter-Strike and Valorant. The curves are your own peeks; the walk-through's player is scripted to improve.
