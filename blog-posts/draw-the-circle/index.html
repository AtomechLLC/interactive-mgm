How it works

Thirty players have already landed and looted. Click the map to place the next safe zone. The page works out what your placement does to each of them.

Every count is computed from your placement: straight-line walks, a fixed reach before the storm, cover from woods and buildings. The player positions are invented for this page and fixed, and real players would not walk in straight lines. After PUBG, Apex Legends and Fortnite.
