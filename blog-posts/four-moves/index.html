Replanned · Warlock Talent Trees

A pick is worth what the room makes it

How it works

Choose four moves for one Pokémon, then watch it take on five Elite Four rooms in a row. You cannot change moves between rooms. Afterwards, choose again with what you learned.

Nothing is rolled. The type chart and the five teams are real; the damage rule is simplified and printed under the rooms. After the Elite Four of Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen, and the Demonology talents that were strong on paper and worth little in a raid full of healers.
