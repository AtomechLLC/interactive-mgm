The same reward, forty times

How it works

Forty coins appear one at a time: click each. Before you start, place your three variant cards on the strip (a new sound, a rare colour, a bigger payout) by clicking a slot. You only have three.

Every coin appears the same distance from your pointer, so the times compare. The chart is your own clicks; forty clicks from one person is a small sample and may show no drift at all. The walk-through's clicks are scripted and flat by construction. After Super Mario's coins, Breath of the Wild's Korok seeds and Hades' boons.
