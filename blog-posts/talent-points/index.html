How it works

Spend ten points on the canvas, then fight for 30 seconds with keys 1 2 3 4 (or click the spells).

An invented four-spell caster; icons are World of Warcraft's (wow.zamimg.com). Damage values are illustrative. "Best play" is computed for your build by always casting the spell with the most damage per second of casting. After the Burning Crusade Shadow Bolt Warlock and Diablo II's one-skill builds.
