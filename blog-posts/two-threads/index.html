Replanned · Puzzle Dependency Chart

Wire the puzzles, then find out where you get stuck

How it works

Six puzzles, two with narrow locks. Wire which ones wait on which, then play: Space stops the marker. Watch how many threads you have when you miss.

The curve, the misses and the story order are your own playthrough. The count of solve orders is exact. The timing lock and the rule that solving widens the rest are stand-ins made for this page. After Ron Gilbert's dependency charts for Monkey Island and Thimbleweed Park.
