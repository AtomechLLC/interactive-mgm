Which Loss Does It Erase?

Mode

Each kind is named by the loss it undoes. The strip on each card runs from before the fight to after the loss, and shows when its trigger comes up. Click a kind to read it; in Sort mode, click the kind that would have erased each loss.

power is measured against the game

Power Is Relative

The journey

A Potion heals 20 HP whether your Pokémon has 20 HP or 180. An item is a trigger when it undoes at least one enemy hit, so a Pokémon about to faint survives the next blow.

HP: Gen III+ formula, IV 15, no EVs. Potion 20, Super 60, Hyper 120 HP (Gen VII on); Max Potion heals fully. Pokémon art: The Pokémon Company, via PokeAPI sprites.
