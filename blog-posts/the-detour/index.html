A fishing hole in a speed run

How it works

Sprint to the flag as fast as you can: Space hops the hurdles. There is a fishing hole on the way. You run it three times, and the rule at the pond changes each time. Rate each run when it ends.

At the pond: F starts or stops fishing, Space strikes when the float dips. Whether you fish, how long you stay and how you rate the run are your own; the walk-through's ratings belong to a scripted player and are only there to fill the table. After Big the Cat's fishing in Sonic Adventure, the Mako in Mass Effect and Yakuza's side games.
