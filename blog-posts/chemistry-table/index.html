How it works

Seven one-line rules for fire, water, lightning, metal and wind. Click rules on or off, then run three scenes that were built before your rules existed.

Every outcome is simulated on the grid from the rules you switched on. The three scenes and the line between surprising and degenerate are this page's own. After the chemistry of Breath of the Wild, Noita and Divinity: Original Sin 2.
