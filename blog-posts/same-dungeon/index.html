How it works

Each run: pick an affix, click packs in the order you will fight them until you have 60 enemy forces, then go. Beat 5:00.

Pack times and danger are illustrative, and shown on each pack so you can plan like a route planner. Bolstering, Sanguine, Explosive and Volcanic are simplified from World of Warcraft's Mythic+ affixes; Crimson and Ancient are invented cosmetic ones. The five routes drawn at the bottom are your own.
