All of Brian Feeney’s Items

Every item on Brian Feeney’s D&D Magic Items Megasheet, quoted as he wrote it. Search by name or by what an item does (try “fall”, “door”, “invisible” or “breath”). Each item has its own illustration; switch to Gallery to browse them as pictures and click one to read it. Gold items are the twenty played out on Same Problem, New Answers. Purple notes read each item through a lens from the course’s Game Design Scriptures.

Items: Brian Feeney (Riot Games), D&D Magic Items Megasheet, Core Set, quoted with his spelling. Illustrations: game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0) by Lorc, Delapouite and other contributors; the pick of icon for each item, and the effect badge read from its description, are this page’s own. Designer’s notes: this page’s reading of each item through the course’s Game Design Scriptures; they are not Brian’s words. The rarity tag shows only Rare, Uncommon and Artifact; the sheet’s other entries in that column are working notes.
