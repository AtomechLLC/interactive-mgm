case study: Legends of Runeterra

Unbalanced Meta: Elusives

A keyword that could only be answered by itself, and what that did to the whole game.

Elusive units could only be blocked by other Elusive units. They were cheap and hit hard for their cost, so the only real answers were your own Elusives or removal spells. And because Elusives lived mostly in one region, the problem could only counter itself.

the meta

The only counter is itself

Patch:

    A toy model: each week, players drift toward the decks that won last week. The win rates are illustrative, not real ladder data.

    Ground decks can’t block Elusives, so they bleed out. The only things that survive are more Elusive decks and spell-heavy decks that can remove them. Two archetypes, one keyword. Try the fixes: making Elusives weaker helps a little; giving every region its own answer brings the whole field back.
    design takeaway

    When a mechanic is its own only answer

    • Count the answers. For every strong mechanic, list what beats it. If the list is “the same mechanic”, the meta will fold into it.
    • Check where the answers live. An answer locked in the same region (faction, class, colour) as the threat forces everyone into that region.
    • Price evasion honestly. Unblockable damage is worth more than its stats suggest. Cheap and hard-hitting is the dangerous combo.
    • Spread the counters. Give other factions their own way to interact, so players have a choice of how to answer.
