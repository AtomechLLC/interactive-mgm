One pool, two spenders: heal or strike?

How the Knight spends SOUL

After Hollow Knight (Team Cherry, 2017): nail hits fill SOUL, and 33 SOUL buys either a Focus (one mask back, standing still) or a Vengeful Spirit. Numbers are illustrative: a 300 HP boss, nail 5, spell 15, 11 SOUL per hit, a 2.4 s opening, then a 0.8 s telegraphed attack. The Knight dodges every attack except every third, and a Focus still charging when an attack lands is broken.
