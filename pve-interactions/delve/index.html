Unavoidable Calamity

Designed by Alonso R Baselli, Diogo Guerreiro and Pavle Sivakov

Void cultists smuggled corrupted wine through a cavern under Silvermoon. The caged animals drank it first. Twenty-three pulls, an elite, a boss, and a barrel to break in every room: every pull built so that “DPS them down” is not the answer.

Their enemies, encounters, solutions and map, played here as written. Icons: World of Warcraft (Blizzard Entertainment). Numbers are this page’s tuning.

                Every Pull, Two Ways

                Player

                Each bar is one simulated fight from sim.js. The solution player follows the board’s kill order, interrupts and stuns the casts the board names, dispels, dodges telegraphs and uses the Void Collector. The DPS player hits the nearest enemy and ignores every mechanic. Both have the same health, damage and potions.
