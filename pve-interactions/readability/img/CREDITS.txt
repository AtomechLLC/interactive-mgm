Into the Breach (Subset Games, 2018). Every image in this folder is the game's own art, used here as course material.

ui/        tiles (tiles_grass), structures, telegraph arrows, stripes and icons, copied unchanged from the game's
           resource.dat (unpacked with C:\Projects\GameDesignSkills\IntoTheBreach\unpack_resources.py, img/combat/).

weapon files), as summarised in IntoTheBreach\unit_behaviour_rules.md with file:line citations.
