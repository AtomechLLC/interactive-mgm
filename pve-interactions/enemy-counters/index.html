fundamentals for designing engaging PvE interactions

One opposition, many decisions

Ideally, the player has a variety of different decisions to overcome the opposition. Mario has two verbs, jump and fireball, and three enemies that each ask a different question of them.

the PvE interaction model

Start, opposition, goal

The level is the whole loop: Mario starts on the left, the flagpole is the goal, and the enemies are the opposition in between. The rules say what each verb does to each enemy, and every enemy is a decision: jump or fireball?
