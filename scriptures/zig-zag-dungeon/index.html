The Zig-Zag Dungeon

Ten rooms, one hero, and you choose the order. Hazards are negatives, won fights and reward chests are positives, and campfires bring things back to neutral. The same ten rooms can make a wave that keeps growing or a run that goes numb.

Dan Felder's Structure of Fun (Entry 22): alternating, escalating positives and negatives, until the experience completes in success or defeat. Contrast is impact, so repetition loses its power, and a smooth climb leaves players tense and numb. This is a toy model. A room that points the same way as the room before it lands at half strength unless it is three times the size, which is a pattern-breaker. Hazards and fights add strain and a campfire clears it. A fight is lost when strain is above 6 going in, or when the hero lacks the tool it needs. Sprites: Pixel Art Atlas (Angais, CC0), AI-generated.
