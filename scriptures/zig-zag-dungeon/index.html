The Zig-Zag Dungeon

Eight rooms, one hero, and you choose the order. Every fight raises stress and leaves fatigue behind. A campfire lets both drop. Arrange the rooms and watch what shape the run takes.

Felder's Structure of Fun (Entry 22): a fun experience creates an oscillating, escalating zig-zag of success and stress, until it completes in success or defeat, rather than a constant climb that slowly tires the player. The numbers here are a toy model: a fight costs hearts for its own stress plus the fatigue carried into it, and a campfire restores three hearts and most of the fatigue.
