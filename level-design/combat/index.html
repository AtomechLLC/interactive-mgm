Saltworks: a study of Dust II

What we borrowed

Reference studied: Dust II, from Counter-Strike (Valve). Saltworks borrows its structure only: three lanes, a long sightline, a doorway in the middle, connectors and set-back spawns. The layout is our own, mirrored for team deathmatch, and drawn with this section’s floor-plan kit. One tile is 40 px.
