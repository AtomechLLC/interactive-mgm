Pick a tool

Click the arena or press U to use the tool. The coloured band on the timeline is when it works.

heal vs shield

Healing erases mistakes. Shields invite risk.

Heal after

The ally waits out every swing, darts in, darts back. A slip gets healed, but nothing changes how they play.

Shield first

The shield is a call to action: the ally steps in and swings freely, and the enemy backs off to wait it out.

reactive: heal and resurrect

Reactive tools need the event first

Heal

Before it is useful, an enemy has to hit the ally.

Revive

The same idea with a harder condition: the ally has to die first.

the same punch, on yourself

Iron Flesh, block, Estus

Proactive

“I cast a spell that reduces the damage I take by 50%.” Cast before the punch.

Coactive

“I block a punch I see coming and take 50% less damage.” Timed to the punch.

Reactive

“I take the hit, then heal up afterwards.” After the punch.

healing + intervention + shields

Stack them on one hit: damage reduced

A shield before, an intervention during and a heal after, all anchored on the same event. Each tool covers a different moment, so together they form a rich toolkit for handling enemy attacks.
