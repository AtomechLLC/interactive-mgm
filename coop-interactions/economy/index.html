in summary

Game Economies Run on Scarcity

Watch out for

Unusable resources and previous-tier overflow: gear the owner can’t use ends up handed to someone it flattens
Unlimited exchanges: any trade with no cap or cost gets repeated until it breaks something
99Zero scenarios: a level 99 player farming a level 1 dungeon and selling the loot for more than their own content pays

Require

＋Taps: know every place a resource enters the game
−Sinks: know every place it leaves, and whether the sinks keep pace with the taps
Scarcity: an economy succeeds or fails on it. A free market only works if the system has resilience.

An auction house

Speeds up every trade, so exploits and inflation spread quickly
Only means something if scarcity exists
Undermines progression when buying is easier than playing
Is worthless when nothing on it beats what play gives you
Works best inside a closed system
