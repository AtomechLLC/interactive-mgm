teamplay sequencing framework

Setup, Exploit, Payoff

Four players, four elements, one enemy: every stage leaves a hook for the next.

collaboration hooks

Only the Next Piece Fits the Hook

A setup leaves a state on the enemy, and that state has a shape. Only the abilities built for that shape can plug into it, so the order of play is part of the design.

by 1 player vs by 3 players

Same Chain, Different Clock

One kit can hold the whole chain, but cooldowns stretch it out and the Wet state nearly runs out. Split across three players, the same chain lands in a third of the time, as long as they time the hand-off.

why payoffs need setups

The Payoff Lands When Held ≥ Windup

Payoffs are strong because they are slow. A charged beam needs the target to stay put for its whole windup, so it needs someone else to hold the enemy still for at least that long.

advanced forms

Setup A + Setup B → Payoff

Water and Chill can land in either order, but both have to land before the Rock. Add an exploit between them and the payoff grows: a pull gathers the enemies before the shatter.
