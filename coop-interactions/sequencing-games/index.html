Sequencing in Real Games

The same setup, exploit and payoff, in Mario, Pokémon, World of Warcraft and a dozen clips.

one-two combo · Super Mario Bros.

Luigi Stomps, Mario Kicks

A walking Koopa hurts whoever touches it. A stomp turns it into a shell, a state that is harmless on its own, and the kick turns that shell into a projectile that clears the whole row.

setup → payoff · Pokémon doubles

Rain Makes Thunder Never Miss

Thunder is strong but only 70% accurate. In rain it always hits. In a double battle, Squirtle spends its turn on Rain Dance so that Pikachu’s next ten Thunders all land.

setup A + setup B + setup C → payoff · Classic World of Warcraft

Three Debuffs, Then the Payoff

Sunder Armor, Faerie Fire and Curse of Recklessness do almost no damage. Each one only lowers the boss’s armor, and together they make every physical hit from the rest of the raid land harder.
