Where You Meet Each One

Equivalent

Value in = value out. Nothing is lost, even when the count changes.

1 apple → 0.5 orange, when an orange is worth 2 apples
5 minutes of play → 100 coins, at the same rate every time

Design note: value moves between players, so who holds it matters, not how much exists.

Growth

More comes out than went in. The trade creates value.

Group XP bonus: killing together pays more than killing alone
CRClash Royale card donation: the giver earns gold and XP, the receiver keeps the card

Design note: growth trades need caps, or two friends can trade back and forth forever.

Erosive

Less comes out than went in. The trade burns value.

Auction house cut: 5% of every sale leaves the economy
Boosting a friend: 100 coins of your time buys 50 coins of theirs

Design note: erosion is a sink. It stops infinite boosting and slows inflation.
