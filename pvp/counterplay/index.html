Counterplay

The act of responding to an opponent’s actions with options in your toolkit that beat theirs.

SystemicBuilt into the core rules. Everyone has it, all the time.
SituationalOpens up only when the game state sets it up.
SpecializedA tool, weapon or move you pick that answers specific threats.
