Who wins the trade?

Armor only reduces damage. You still flinch, so your swing is lost.

Super armor only prevents hit stun. You pay the full health cost, but your swing lands.

Immunity prevents both. Hits simply don’t land.

Three Dials, Three Kinds of Outcome

ImmunityBinary: all or nothingEasy to understand. Dodge rolls, get-ups, high-investment supers.Hits simply don’t land.
Super armorAnalog: damage, no reactionGives slow, weighty moves a leg up over fast ones. Still attackable, not interruptible.Counterplay: throws and multi-hits.
ArmorAnalog: less damage, still reactsVery specific balance tuning. Stat buffs and long transformations.More powerful, but you still have to pay attention.

Hit stun and damage are the two costs of a strike. These techniques let you, the designer, tune each one separately.
