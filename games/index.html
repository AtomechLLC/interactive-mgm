Games Referenced

Every game the MGM and DGMS pages, GIFs and deck notes point to, plus the blog posts. Search for a game, or pick a course or module to see what it draws on. Open a game to see where it comes up.

Mentions count each time a game, a common short name (WoW, BotW, SF6) or one of its signature characters, bosses or items (Pikachu, a Goomba, a Karazhan boss, a League champion) appears in a page, a GIF caption, the deck notes or the module proposals. They show how much a module leans on a game, not how many slides it fills. A game shown only in a picture, with no caption or notes, is not counted. Phalanx and Forsaken are Atomech’s own projects. “Mario (series)” gathers Mario characters and enemies named without a game; named games such as Super Mario World have their own entries.

Built from the page catalog, the pages themselves, the module proposals and the decks’ speaker notes.
