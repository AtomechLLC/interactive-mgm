8 · Reward with care

The longer and brighter the tell, the bigger the threat can be

One monster, three attacks, then a fourth that breaks the rule. Each one is plotted as tell length against damage.

The principle

Punish fairly and with warning. The longer and brighter the tell, the bigger the threat can be. The more counterplay something has, the stronger it can be.

Timings and damage are illustrative. Source decks: Satisfaction, Motivation.
