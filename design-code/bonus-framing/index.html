9 · Balance for purpose, not equality

Frame mechanics as bonuses, not penalties

World of Warcraft’s rest system: one four hour session under two labels. Count the XP on each side.

The principle

Frame mechanics as bonuses. Players hate losses about twice as much as they like equivalent gains, so the same numbers can read as a punishment or as a gift.

The story as Blizzard’s designers have told it: early testers met a system that cut XP after long play and hated it. The team doubled the XP needed, called the old full rate “rested” at 200% and the old reduced rate “normal”, and players welcomed it.
