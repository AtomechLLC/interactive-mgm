9 · Balance for purpose, not equality

A 50% win rate proves nothing

Twenty matches each for two heroes, drawn as the gold lead over time. Both finish at exactly 50%.

The principle

Balance means alignment with the ideal game session, not equality. A 50% win rate proves nothing. The right balance target depends on your goal.

The matches are generated from a fixed seed and are illustrative, not data from a shipped game. Source deck: Proven Game Balancing Techniques.
