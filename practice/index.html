Self-Study & Workshops

The hands-on half of the course. Live workshops drill one design skill in class; the self-study project applies the same skill to your own game afterward. Both follow the same days, and every day links the interactives that teach it.

Each row pairs a day’s workshop with its self-study part. The round icons open the interactive pages for that day’s topic.

How the two paths fit

One skill, twice

The workshop practices a skill on a shared example (Breath of the Wild without gliding, nine animals, a DOOM room). The self-study applies it to your team’s game. Day 5 drills A → B logic in class; Part 5 uses it to grow new abilities for your game.

One game, start to finish

The self-study parts stack: Part 1 picks three concepts, Part 3 commits to one, and every later part adds a section to your design document. Part 17 folds them into a master GDD and a vertical slice.
