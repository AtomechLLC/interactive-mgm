Self-Study Project

Seventeen parts that grow one game, from a team argument about what to make to a vertical slice. Each part has the exercise, a worked example from the course’s own sample game, Echidna Z, and the interactives to open while you work. Tick a part off when you post it; your progress stays in this browser.

How each part works

  1. Do the exercise with your team (up to 4 people), or alone.
  2. Use the blank template if it helps: every one is optional.
  3. Post the result to your Discord thread and tag the instructors.
  4. Fold what you learn into your game design document as you go.

Echidna Z, the running example

Keba wanted a game about echidnas; Alex wanted heavily animated combat. The templates follow their “playful soulslike” from that first disagreement to a full design document, so every part shows a finished answer next to the blank one.

Open “Worked example” on any part to see it.
