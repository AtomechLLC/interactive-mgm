Live Workshops

Seventeen in-class drills, one per course day. Each lists its goal, the instructor’s example, the framework to fill in on Miro, the task with its group size, time and zoom level, and the interactives to open on the shared screen.

Running a workshop

  1. Show the goal and the instructor’s example.
  2. Walk through the framework and open the matching Miro board.
  3. Set the timer and let groups work the task.
  4. Each group picks a presenter; the class gives feedback.

Zoom levels

Each drill works at one zoom: a single action, a feature, an encounter, the whole game project, the team system, or open creative work. Filter the drills by zoom with the bar above.
