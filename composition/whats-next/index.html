Challenges First, Then the Tools

Slide 266’s loop on one stretch of ground. Each tool enters the ledger only after a challenge asks for it; the fireball arrives with no challenge, so it is cut until a Spiny gives it one.

Sprites: Super Mario Bros. (Nintendo). The four steps are slide 266’s; the Mario example is this page’s.

Out-Ranged in Lane, Then Wind Wall

League of Legends. The problem: a melee champion facing a mage who hits from far away. Every tool Yasuo gets answers that problem.

Icons and portraits: League of Legends (Riot Games), from Data Dragon. The order of the steps is this page’s illustration of the loop, not a record of how Riot designed Yasuo.

A Fire Gym, Then a Water Team

Pokémon. The problem: a gym whose Charmander burns through a Grass team. The route before it hands the player the answer.

Art: Pokémon (Nintendo, Game Freak, The Pokémon Company). Rain rules are from Generation II on: Water moves ×1.5, Fire moves ×0.5, Thunder never misses. Damage numbers are illustrative.

A Raid-Wide Blast, Then the Heals

World of Warcraft. The problem: a boss whose Inferno hits all twenty raiders at once. Each tool is a healer’s or a paladin’s answer to it.

Spell icons: World of Warcraft (Blizzard Entertainment). The boss, its Inferno and the numbers are illustrative.

make the problem first · tower defense

Runners Leak Through, Then the Towers

The problem: a wave too fast to stop by hand. Each tower is placed because a wave asked for it, and a tower with no wave is cut.

Icons: game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0): sprint, heavy-helm, bat-wing, bow-arrow, frozen-orb, unlit-bomb, rocket, castle. Speeds and lives are illustrative.

make the problem first · deckbuilder

A 12-Damage Hit, Then the Cards

A deckbuilder in the style of Slay the Spire. The problem: an enemy that shows it will hit for 12 next turn. Each card added to the deck answers that hit.

Cards are this page’s own. Icons: game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0): ogre, swordman, round-shield, pointy-sword, broken-shield, poison-bottle, heavy-helm. Numbers are illustrative.
